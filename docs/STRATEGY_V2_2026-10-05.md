# Strategy V2 research expansion — 5 October 2026

Simulation/research only. No broker order, API key, funding, or live-mode change is part of this revision.

## Crypto
- Kraken EUR universe discovery now inspects every currently online EUR spot pair through public endpoints.
- Fiat and stablecoin bases are excluded. Candidates are ranked by current 24h EUR turnover before strategy P/L is calculated.
- Discovery observed 497 eligible EUR pairs during the manual check on 5 October 2026.
- The research set is capped at 12 assets with at least 600 closed daily sessions and a common calendar. The observed set was XBT, XRP, SUI, ETH, SOL, ADA, NEAR, ZEC, QNT, LTC, TAO and LINK versus EUR.
- This current-liquidity screen still has survivorship/listing bias and cannot validate future profitability.

## Strategy templates
- Four slow trend/breakout templates are expanded to eight predeclared templates.
- Two faster 10/50-day variants use 10/20-day breakouts, 8% position stops and 30/45-day maximum holds to test higher-volatility moves.
- Intermediate 30/120 variants are added. Existing 20/100 and 50/200 controls remain.
- Portfolio drawdown guard remains 5%, there is no arbitrary simultaneous-position-count cap; entries are bounded by available simulated capital, lot size, liquidity and per-entry allocation (20 on 100 simulated capital), and stressed costs remain mandatory.

## Stocks / ETFs
- Diagnostic universe is expanded from SOFI/U to SOFI, U, PLUG, OPEN, FUBO, NIO, MARA and RIOT.
- The basket is fixed before simulation and deliberately includes more volatile/liquid lower-price equities. Unit price is not treated as valuation.
- Existing whole-share, volume, corporate-action, calendar, fee, slippage and no-live-order guards remain.

## Verification
- 69 focused crypto/research/equity tests pass; one optional-calendar test is skipped when its pinned local dependency is absent.
- Broad Kraken collection was exercised against public endpoints: 12 assets, 720 common daily sessions.
- Full broad strategy simulation is intentionally not claimed as completed in this note. No profitability claim is made until the expanded experiment finishes under a frozen protocol.

## Multi-position revision
- No arbitrary position-count ceiling is applied by the V2 research engine for either crypto or equities.
- The engine may hold every instrument in the declared research universe when signals qualify.
- Position count is therefore an outcome, not a target. Available cash, exchange/broker lot minimums, liquidity, fees and the 5% portfolio drawdown halt still constrain feasible exposure.
- This change does not imply that more positions improve returns; the expanded simulation must measure that.

## Freqtrade dry-run activation profile
- `max_open_trades=-1`: Freqtrade no longer imposes a position-count ceiling; the pair universe and available simulated cash are the practical bounds.
- Static stake reduced from 20 EUR to 10 EUR so a 100 EUR wallet can express more simultaneous signals without pretending that tiny trades are free.
- Only 90% of simulated balance is tradable, leaving a fee/cost reserve.
- The live dry-run pairlist is expanded to the 12 liquid/history-screened EUR pairs observed by the V2 discovery.
- Current strategy ROI target starts at 3%, versus the configured 0.8% fee per side (about 1.6% round trip before spread/slippage). This is only a coarse cost hurdle, not a forecast of net profit.
- News/sentiment is not used as an entry trigger in this deployment. It requires timestamped, reproducible inputs before it can be tested without look-ahead bias.
